About IF Cantten

The Informatics Engineering Canteen at ITS offers a modern, semi-open dining space with minimalist industrial design, surrounded by greenery for a refreshing atmosphere.

With its clean, comfortable environment and contemporary architecture marked by the iconic “IF” sign, it’s an ideal spot for students to eat, study, or relax — making it one of the most aesthetic and functional canteens on campus.
